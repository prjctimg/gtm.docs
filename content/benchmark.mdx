Automated measurements of gtm's resource usage during playback, charted
release-over-release and compared against the reference CLI player
[cliamp](https://github.com/bjarneo/cliamp).

## Before you read any of the numbers

Take this with a grain of salt. Like all benchmarks, these checks measure one
narrow harness on one machine — a fixed FLAC and MP3 fixture, a headless
daemon, no audio device. They say nothing definitive about your library, your
machine, or your ears.

The honest framing: this is for fun — a friendly compare of how gtm, a young
terminal player, fares against the more feature-rich and mature **cliamp**.
cliamp ships a lot more than gtm does. If gtm holds its own on memory and
startup latency, great — and the moment it does not, the regression shows up
in these charts too. That is the point of tracking this over time instead of
cherry-picking a single run.

The page is a living document: it renders whatever the committed `stats.json`
contains. cliamp reference runs appear automatically whenever the benchmark
harness records them.
