## Disclaimer 

This is purely for fun and educational purposes only — a friendly comparison of how gtm, a young
terminal player, fares against the more feature-rich and mature **cliamp**.
cliamp ships a lot more than gtm does and that's great because feature parity is not the ultimate goal.

If gtm holds its own on memory and startup latency, great — and the moment it does not, the regression shows up
in these charts too.

Like all benchmarks, these checks measure one narrow harness on one machine — a fixed FLAC and MP3 fixture, a headless
daemon, no audio device. They say nothing definitive about your library, your machine, (or your ears). 
There's a lot more involved in setting things up and I decided to just take the naive route because its enough, at least for now. 


Take this with a grain of salt.
