## Subscribing

Add a podcast by its **RSS/Atom feed URL**:

- `Alt+p` opens the Podcast Feeds picker, where you add a feed by URL and manage
  subscriptions
- Inside the picker, press `/` to search the **public podcast directory** (the
  iTunes Search API — no account, no API key). An empty query is legal and
  returns what is popular; results are cached per query, so re-entering the
  picker with the same term costs nothing. A search replaces the subscription
  list for as long as it has results, and `Esc` goes back to your
  subscriptions.
- `Enter` on a search result **subscribes** to that feed. On the subscription
  list, `Enter` opens episodes, `a` adds by URL and `r` refreshes.
- CLI manages subscriptions:

```bash
gtm --cli podcast add <feed-url>
gtm --cli podcast list
gtm --cli podcast remove <feed_id>
gtm --cli podcast episodes <feed_id>
gtm --cli podcast refresh            # refresh all feeds
gtm --cli podcast refresh <feed_id>  # refresh one feed
gtm --cli podcast play <feed_id> <episode_index>
gtm --cli podcast status
```

Feeds are parsed by the daemon and their episodes listed in the podcast
category of the library.

There is **no** `gtm --cli podcast search` — discovery is a TUI picker feature
only. Subscriptions are manageable from the CLI as below.

Feed constraints:

- **30-second** fetch timeout (`FETCH_TIMEOUT`)
- **8 MiB** maximum feed body (`MAX_FEED_BYTES`) — larger feeds are
  rejected as suspicious

Subscription state persists in `podcast.json` in the daemon config dir, so
feeds survive restarts.

## Browsing & playback

Episode lists show title, date, and duration, plus `[no audio]` and
`[transcript]` marks where they apply. `t` fetches an episode's transcript into
the lyrics pane. Episodes play like any other remote source
(`podcast://<feed_id>/<index>`); the stream is decoded through the same remote
pipeline.

### Artwork

Feeds and episodes carry artwork. gtm reads channel art from
`<itunes:image href>` or Atom `<logo>`/`<icon>`, and upsizes the directory's
100 px thumbnails to 600 px by path. Artwork from the directory survives
subscribing, so a show whose feed publishes no image of its own still has one.

---

## Storage

Episodes are streamed on demand — the daemon does not silently download
every episode. Downloaded episodes (from queue/streams you pull) land in
the audio library and are indexed there.
